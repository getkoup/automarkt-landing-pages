What is the ceramic coating offer?

Sleek Detailing is offering a 10-year ceramic coating for only $699. Call or text (561) 236-6091 with your vehicle details to confirm eligibility, package inclusions, and availability.

What preparation does my car need?

Sleek Detailing’s process includes a foam pre-soak, wheel cleaning, gentle hand wash, clay bar decontamination, iron remover treatment, and de-ionized water rinse. Paint correction may be recommended depending on the condition of your finish. The team will explain what is included in the $699 package and any additional work before you book.

Will it prevent scratches and rock chips?

Ceramic coating helps protect against environmental contaminants and makes cleaning easier, but it does not make paint scratch-proof or prevent rock chips. Ask Sleek Detailing about paint protection film if chip protection is your priority.

What does “10-year” mean for care and warranty?

It describes the coating package in this offer. Actual performance depends on use, exposure, and maintenance. Ask the team for the specific coating product, written warranty terms, and any required upkeep when booking.

How do I care for my coated vehicle?

Follow the studio’s curing and washing guidance for your coating. Regular, gentle washing helps maintain the finish; avoid abrasive cleaning and remove contaminants promptly. Sleek Detailing will explain aftercare at pickup.
