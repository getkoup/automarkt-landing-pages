What is a spray-in bed liner?+

It’s a protective coating applied to a prepared truck bed. It follows the bed’s shape and creates a textured surface to help reduce scratches, scuffs, and everyday wear from hauling.

How much will it cost for my truck?+

Pricing depends on your truck, bed size, condition, and the preparation needed. Call MOB for a vehicle-specific quote and to confirm how the up to 30% off offer applies to your service.

How soon can I use my truck bed?+

Application and curing times vary by product and preparation. The team will confirm your drop-off, pickup, and when you can safely start loading the bed again.

Can you line a used or previously lined bed?+

The existing surface needs to be assessed first. Rust, damage, or a previous liner may require extra preparation or removal. Let the team know what’s currently in your truck bed when requesting a quote.

Spray-in or drop-in: what’s the difference?+

A spray-in liner bonds to the prepared bed surface. A drop-in liner is a separate insert. The right choice depends on the finish, fit, and intended use. Ask the team which approach makes sense for your truck.
