MORE THAN A BEAUTIFUL FINISH

A layer of glass.
A whole lot of shine.

Ceramic coating is a clear, liquid nano-ceramic that cures into a hard, flexible glass shield on your paint. It works like an extra clear coat that makes everyday care easier.

9H surface hardness

Helps stop bird droppings, bugs, tar, tree sap, and road grime from bonding to your paint.

Ultimate deep gloss

Nano particles fill the smallest pores in the paint for a lasting, showroom-style shine.

Hydrophobic. Self-cleaning.

Water beads up and rolls off, carrying dirt with it, so washes are faster and easier.

Never wax again

A durable layer that replaces waxing and keeps salt air, sun, and pollen off your finish.

Ceramic coating is not scratch-proof and does not prevent rock chips. Regular hand or touchless washing still matters.

What is the current ceramic coating offer?

Our 5-year ceramic coating is $499 for a limited time. Call or text (910) 901-7361 to confirm your vehicle's eligibility and book.

Will ceramic coating prevent every scratch or rock chip?

No. It helps resist light scratches, swirl marks, chemicals, and contamination, but it is not scratch-proof and won't stop rock chips. For chip protection, ask us about paint protection film.

My car has scratches and swirls. Can it still be coated?

Yes. We prep the paint before coating, and minor swirls and scratches can be corrected first so they aren't sealed in. We'll tell you what your paint needs on your quote.

How do I maintain the coating?

Hand wash or use a touchless car wash. Avoid tunnel car washes with brushes, since they put swirl marks in the coating. We also offer maintenance washes for our ceramic customers.

Can you coat my car at my home?

No. Ceramic coating is done only in our Wilmington shop, so paint prep and curing happen in a controlled environment. Just drop your car off. We'll work around your schedule.
