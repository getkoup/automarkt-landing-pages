Ceramic coating FAQ

How does the up to 30% off offer work?

What is ceramic coating?

A liquid coating that bonds to your car's paint and cures into a hard, glossy protective layer. It adds shine, makes washing easier and helps protect against UV, bird droppings, sap and road grime.

How long does it last?

Our Silver, Gold and Platinum packages offer 1, 5 and 10 years of durability. Regular hand washing and the aftercare kit help it last its full term. Warranty terms are confirmed with your quote.

Do I need paint correction first?

If your paint has light swirls or haze, single-stage paint correction is included in every package. Coating over swirls would lock them in, so we fix them first.

How long will my car be at the shop?

It depends on your vehicle's size, its paint condition and the package you choose. We'll confirm the timing when you book.

Is ceramic coating the same as PPF?

No. Paint Protection Film (PPF) is a thick, clear film that protects against rock chips. Ceramic coating is a thin, hard layer for gloss and easy cleaning. Many owners combine both. Ask us about pairing them.
