Straight answers

Houston PPF questions

What does this offer include?
Full front end PPF for $1,299 (regularly $2,000): front bumper, hood, front fenders and side mirrors. Our team confirms the exact coverage and fit for your vehicle before booking.
How long does the install take?
It depends on the size of the vehicle, the paint condition and the coverage. Full front packages generally take one to two days. We never rush the install, because precision is what makes it look clean and last.
How long does PPF last?
Premium paint protection film can last between 5 and 10 years when properly maintained.
Can I see the film after it's installed?
PPF is virtually invisible. Most people find it difficult to spot once it's on, and your original finish stays glossy underneath. It can hide very minor imperfections, but deeper scratches should be corrected before install.
Can PPF be removed?
Yes. When it's professionally installed and removed, PPF comes off without damaging the factory paint.
PPF or ceramic coating?
They protect against different things. PPF stops rock chips, road debris, scratches and impacts. Ceramic coating guards against UV, water spots, dirt and contaminants. Many customers choose both, with the coating applied over the film for extra gloss and easier washing.
