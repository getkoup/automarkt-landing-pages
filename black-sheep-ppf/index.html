What is Paint Protection Film?

Paint Protection Film (PPF) is a clear, durable film applied over your car's painted surfaces. Think of it as a screen protector for your paint.

It absorbs the impact of rock chips, road debris, bug splatter and light scratches. Your paint underneath stays clean, glossy and protected, which helps keep your car looking new and holding its value.

What is Paint Protection Film (PPF)?

A clear, durable film applied over your car's paint. It protects against rock chips, road debris, bug splatter and light scratches while keeping your paint's color and gloss.

Does the film really self-heal?

Yes, for light swirls and fine scratches in the film's top coat. They fade with heat from the sun or warm water. Deep cuts that go through the film won't heal, but your paint underneath stays protected.

Which package should I choose?

High Traffic Areas covers the front bumper, partial hood, partial fenders and common wear spots like mirrors and door edges, where most rock chips happen. Full Body covers every exterior painted surface, plus headlights and ceramic coating on glass, plastic and rims.
