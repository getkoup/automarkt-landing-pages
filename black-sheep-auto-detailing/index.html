Our premium wash process

How we detail your car

Every exterior detail follows the same careful steps, so you get the same result every time.

  1. Hand and foam wash

    Thick foam loosens dirt before a gentle hand wash, reducing the risk of swirls.

  2. Wheel and tire cleaning

    Brake dust and grime removed from wheels, tires and wheel wells.

  3. Door jamb and gas cap care

    The spots most washes skip get cleaned too.

  4. Spot-free drying

    Careful drying that leaves no water spots behind.

  5. Spray wax protection

    A layer of shine and protection that helps water bead off.

  6. Crystal-clear windows

    Streak-free glass inside and out for a clear view of the road.
