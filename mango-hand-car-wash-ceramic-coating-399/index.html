PROTECTION FOR HOUSTON DRIVING

Houston weather
is hard on paint.

Sun, humidity, rain, pollen and construction dust take a toll. Ceramic coating adds a protective layer to help keep your paint cleaner and glossier.

Sun protection

A durable layer against sun exposure and oxidation.

Water beads off

Water beads and rolls off the coated surface.

Easier washing

Less dirt sticks, so routine cleaning takes less effort.

More gloss

Added depth and clarity for a smooth, glossy finish.

Ceramic coating does not stop rock chips or repair deep scratches.

MANGO’S CERAMIC COATING PROCESS

Prep. Polish.
Then coat.

  1. Prep the paint

    Hand wash, clay bar and iron removal to clean the surface.

  2. Polish for its condition

    Mango chooses a light polish or correction based on the paint.

  3. Apply the coating

    A prep wipe removes residue before the coating goes on.

This is Mango’s general process. Call to confirm what prep and polishing are included at $399.

How do I care for the coating?

Use pH-neutral soap and avoid abrasive mitts or towels. Covered parking helps reduce exposure. Ask the team for aftercare instructions for your coating.

Will it fix chipped paint or deep scratches?

No. Chips and deep scratches need touch-up or body work before coating. Ceramic coating does not stop rock chips.
