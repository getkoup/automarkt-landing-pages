MORE THAN A BEAUTIFUL FINISH

Your paint, the day you bought it.
Every day after.

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds to your paint. Unlike wax that breaks down in weeks, it becomes part of the finish and doesn't peel, fade, or wash off.

A bond that stays

It bonds to your clear coat instead of sitting on top, so it lasts years, not weeks.

Self-healing surface

Minor swirl marks fade with heat exposure, keeping the finish clean and deep.

Water rolls right off

Water, dirt, and bugs bead up and slide away, so your car stays cleaner between washes.

Color that stays vivid

UV protection stops the fading and oxidation that a Texas summer puts on paint.

Ceramic coating doesn't stop rock chips. For chip protection, ask us about paint protection film.

What is the current ceramic coating offer?

Our 10-year ceramic coating is $599 for a limited time. Call or text (737) 399-2779 to confirm your vehicle's eligibility and book.

Is paint prep included?

Yes. Every coating includes our Foundation Prep: triple-soak wash, iron removal, clay bar, primer polish and panel wipe. If your paint needs deeper correction for heavy swirls or scratches, we'll tell you upfront on your quote.

Will ceramic coating stop scratches and rock chips?

No. It resists chemicals, UV and contamination, and minor swirls can fade with heat, but it won't stop rock chips. For chip protection, ask us about paint protection film.

Is the coating warrantied?

Yes. Our coatings come with a Carfax-registered warranty. We'll walk you through the coverage and aftercare for your package when you book.
