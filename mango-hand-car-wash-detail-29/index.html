What’s included in the $29 detail?+

Inside: detailed vacuum, interior surfaces wiped down, door jambs and interior glass cleaned. Outside: wheels and tires cleaned, a hand wash, tire dressing and streak-free exterior glass.

Does it include shampoo, clay bar or sealant?+

No. Shampoo, steam cleaning, clay bar treatment and paint sealant are separate upgrades. Call and we’ll tell you what your car needs.

Will this remove scratches or swirl marks?+

No. A detail won’t remove deep scratches or noticeable swirl marks. Paint correction is the right service for that.

Is this a ceramic coating?+

No. The $29 detail is a cleaning service and does not include wax or ceramic coating. Professional ceramic coating is available from Mango as a separate service.

How long will it take?+

Timing depends on your vehicle’s condition and how busy the shop is. Call before your visit for current availability and a time estimate.

Do I need to bring my car to your shop?+

Yes. All services are performed at 6166 Westheimer Rd, Houston, TX 77057. Open our Google profile for directions ↗.
