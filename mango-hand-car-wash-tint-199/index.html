OUR WINDOW TINT INSTALLATION PROCESS

Care before the film.
Care through the finish.

Mango's installation process covers preparation, fitting, application and a final inspection of your windows.

  1. 01 /

    Existing tint removal

    If old film needs to come off, the team removes it before fitting new tint. Ask about removal requirements and any separate cost.

  2. 02 /

    Glass preparation

    The glass is cleaned to remove dust, residue, oils and contaminants before the new film is applied.

  3. 03 /

    Film cutting

    Each piece of tint is fitted to your vehicle's windows for a clean appearance.

  4. 04 /

    Film installation

    The team applies the film with specialized tools and techniques for a smooth finish.

  5. 05 /

    Final inspection

    Before delivery, every window is checked for appearance, clarity and overall quality.

No. This $199 offer covers side windows and the rear windshield only.

What kinds of window film does Mango offer?
