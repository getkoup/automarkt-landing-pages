What a coating actually does

Three honest reasons people coat their cars

Reason one

Gloss that stays glossy

A cured ceramic layer is optically slicker than clear coat. It deepens the finish the day it goes on, and unlike wax it does not soften, smear or wash off by the next season.

We machine polish the paint before coating, so what gets locked in is a corrected finish, not the swirls it came in with.

Reason two

Wash day gets short

Coated paint is hydrophobic. Water beads and sheets off, and road grime has a much harder time bonding to the surface. Dirt that used to need scrubbing rinses away.

In a Colorado winter that matters twice: mag chloride and slush film wipe off coated paint instead of etching into it.

Reason three

Chemical and UV armor

Bird droppings, bug guts, de icer and high altitude sun all attack clear coat chemically. A ceramic layer takes those hits first and resists etching and oxidation for years.

For rock chips and physical impacts, film is the answer instead. Coating and PPF together is the strongest setup we install, and we quote the combo happily.

Why here, specifically

Colorado is the coating use case

Sun at 6,000 feet

Nearly 300 sunny days a year and thinner air overhead. UV works paint harder up here, and coatings blunt it season after season.

Mag chloride winters

The de icer sprayed on every winter road clings to paint and eats at unprotected clear coat. On coated paint it rinses off.

Dust and hard water

Front Range dust and mineral heavy water leave spots that etch bare clear coat. Coated paint gives them nothing to grab.

Straight answers

Coating questions we hear every week

How long does a ceramic coating last?
Our package is a 10 year ceramic coating, applied in two layers over properly prepped paint. Real world lifespan depends on how the vehicle is stored, washed and maintained, and we hand you the simple routine that keeps it performing at pickup.
Is it scratch proof?
No, and anyone who says otherwise is selling something. A coating resists chemical etching, UV and light wash marring. It does not stop rock chips or door dings. That job belongs to paint protection film, and the two together is the strongest setup we install.
Do I still have to wash the car?
Yes, just less often and much faster. Dirt bonds poorly to coated paint, so most washes become a rinse and a quick contact wash. What you can skip forever is waxing.
Why does the prep cost more than the bottle?
Because the coating locks in whatever is underneath it. Decontamination and machine polishing are most of the labor, and they are the difference between sealing in corrected paint and sealing in swirls. It is also why a professional coating outperforms the same idea from a spray bottle.
Can you coat over paint protection film?
Yes, and it is a great combination. The film handles impacts, the coating adds gloss and makes the whole vehicle easier to wash. Ask about the combo in the form and we will quote both together.
How long is the vehicle in the shop?
Usually a day or two depending on the condition of the paint and the package. The coating then continues curing for the first days on the road, and we send you home with simple care instructions for that window.
