How long does the protection last?

Quality materials and precise installation make the difference. Our skilled installers treat your vehicle with exceptional care, giving you peace of mind and PPF protection that can last up to 10 years with proper care.

Does this package help prevent rock chips?

PPF provides a physical barrier that helps reduce chips and scuffs on the covered paint. It cannot prevent every impact. The front windshield ceramic coating is not windshield protection film and does not provide impact protection.
