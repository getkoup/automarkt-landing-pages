    What the film does

    Protection that wax and coatings cannot match

    Ceramic coating makes paint easier to clean and adds gloss. Film is the only thing that physically absorbs impacts, and the two work great together.

    Self healing

    Light swirls and scratches in the film disappear with heat from the sun or warm water.

    Manufacturer backed

    Every install is registered with LLumar and covered by their warranty, honored nationwide.

    Invisible finish

    Computer cut for your exact panels and finished with wrapped edges where the panel allows.

    Protects resale

    Factory paint underneath stays factory. When the film comes off years later, the finish under it looks like day one.

    Straight answers

    PPF questions we hear every week

    What does paint protection film cost?
    It depends on coverage and vehicle size, which is why we quote each vehicle individually. Front bumper coverage is the entry point and full body is the top end. Send the form and you will have exact numbers the same business day.
    How long does the install take?
    Smaller packages are usually done within a day. Full front and full body installs take longer because every panel is prepped and the film is wrapped where panels allow. We confirm timing with your quote.
    Is the film really self healing?
    Yes. The LLumar top coat re flows with heat, so light swirls and wash marks disappear in the sun or with warm water. Deep gouges are a different story, though the film still takes that hit instead of your paint.
    What about the warranty?
    Every install is registered with LLumar and carries their manufacturer backed warranty against yellowing, cracking and peeling. You get the documentation when you pick the vehicle up.
    Can I still ceramic coat the car?
    Yes, and it is a great combination. Coating over film adds gloss and makes washing easier while the film handles impacts. Ask about it in the form and we will quote both together.
