What is the ceramic coating offer?

HP Mobile Detailing is offering a 10-year ceramic coating for only $499. Call or text (682) 465-4893 with your vehicle details to confirm eligibility, package inclusions, and availability.

Where is the coating done?

At our shop. Ceramic coating needs a clean, controlled space to prep and cure properly, so we don’t apply it as a mobile service. We’ll share the shop address and your drop-off time when you book.

What preparation does my car need?

Every coating starts with a thorough wash and paint decontamination. Paint correction may be recommended depending on the condition of your finish. We’ll explain what’s included in the $499 package and any additional work before you book.

Will it prevent scratches and rock chips?

Ceramic coating helps protect against environmental contaminants and makes cleaning easier, but it does not make paint scratch-proof or prevent rock chips. Ask us about paint protection film if chip protection is your priority.

What does “10-year” mean for care and warranty?

It describes the coating package in this offer. Actual performance depends on use, exposure, and maintenance. Ask the team for the specific coating product, written warranty terms, and any required upkeep when booking.

Do you work on all vehicles?

HP works on all makes and models. While we love exotic and luxury vehicles, every customer gets the same care and a clear explanation of the service.

What are your hours?

Monday–Friday 8 AM–6 PM, Saturday 9 AM–5 PM, and Sunday 10 AM–3 PM. Call or text (682) 465-4893 to schedule.
