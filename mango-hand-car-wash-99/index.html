What’s included in the $99 detail?+

Interior wipe-down, crevice blowout, full vacuum and floor mat cleaning, plus a detailed exterior hand wash, inside-and-out window cleaning, tire shine and Hyper Ceramic Wax.

Is this a ceramic coating?+

The $99 package includes Hyper Ceramic Wax for added gloss and paint protection. It is a different service from a professional ceramic coating, which Mango offers separately.

Does the package include seat or carpet shampoo?+

The $99 detail includes vacuuming and interior surface cleaning. Carpet and seat shampoo, deeper interior work and paint correction are separate services. Call to discuss what your car needs.

How long will it take?+

Timing depends on your vehicle’s condition and how busy the shop is. Call before your visit for current availability and a time estimate.
